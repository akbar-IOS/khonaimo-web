# KhonaiMo Web: build notes

## NEEDS AKBAR

1. **Sign in to the app in Chrome once (one click).** The Chrome extension can't act on
   `accounts.google.com`, so the agent can't pick your Google account. A KhonaiMo tab in
   your Chrome is sitting on Google's account chooser. Click your account there (or open
   http://localhost:5173/home → Sign in → Continue with Google). The CLI login earlier
   finished the same way.
